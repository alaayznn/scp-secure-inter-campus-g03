# STRIDE Threat Model
Group {{GG}} | M1 design | Planned checks, not executed attacks

## Method and assets
STRIDE: Spoofing identity, Tampering data, Repudiation of actions, Information disclosure, Denial of service and Elevation of privilege. Likelihood (L) and impact (I) are qualitative Low/Medium/High ratings for the baseline or pre-control state, not measured probabilities. Controls reduce risk but do not prove implementation. Assets: offline Root/Issuing keys, gateway identity and tunnel policy, portal/staff identity, KDC keys/tickets, evidence and repository integrity. Authorized attacker is WAN-only at 172.16.{{G}}.66; tests remain inside this lab.

## Residual risk and priorities
Highest priorities before runtime acceptance: actual isolation/routes, genuine WAN plaintext capture, role-restricted secret placement and resolving replacement-kit acceptance. Baseline has no cryptographic source authentication and remains intentionally observable. Future TLS does not hide metadata; VPN does not protect LAN plaintext; CRL distribution can be blocked; password-based preauthentication can leak an offline guessing oracle. The custom protocol is educational and needs adversarial review; do not present draft design as production assurance.
